Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026

1. Local In-Browser Processing

The KGC OSI - 7 simulation platform operates strictly inside the user's local web browser client. All data framing, bit stuffing, cyclic redundancy check (CRC-16) validation, sliding window state management, and Dijkstra shortest path computations are performed locally. No payload content or simulation data is transmitted to external telemetry servers.

2. Data Collection and Tracking

KGC OSI - 7 does not collect personal identity information, employ tracking pixels, or utilize third-party commercial analytics. UI display mode preferences are saved strictly within local browser storage.

3. Academic & Instructional Context

This software is structured exclusively as a computer networking educational suite for exploring OSI 7-layer protocols. It does not interface with external network interfaces or raw socket hardware.

4. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to revise this policy in accordance with ongoing platform maintenance and standards compliance.
